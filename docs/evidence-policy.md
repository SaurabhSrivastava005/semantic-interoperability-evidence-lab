# Evidence and Outcome Policy
## Unit of assessment
Assess a named implementation or claim for a stated use case, organisation or project, jurisdiction, versions and observation period. Never transfer its outcome automatically to a whole standard or industry.

## Outcome
| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| successful-within-scope | Evidence supports the stated acceptance criteria |
| partially-successful | Some stated objectives met; material gaps remain |
| unsuccessful-for-use-case | Evidence documents failure to meet stated requirements |
| inconclusive | Evidence is insufficient or contradictory |
| not-evaluated | No outcome assessment completed |

Record criteria, observed results and limits. If criteria were not published, say so. Distinguish the source author's reported outcome from SIEL's assessment.

## Source type and review
Record source types separately: official specification, implementer report, vendor case study, independent evaluation, reproducible technical report, authorised contributor experience, or secondary analysis. None automatically establishes correctness.

For each material claim record URL or authorised provenance, publisher, publication date if known, location in the source, access date, relevant versions, author interests, corroborating or conflicting sources and reviewer. Mark unavailable information as not reported.

## Editorial status
draft, submitted, accepted, disputed, superseded or withdrawn.
Accepted means reviewed for publication. It is not certification or independent reproduction.

## Exceptions
Describe expected and observed behaviour, affected scope, consequence and workaround. Classify the reported cause as standard limitation, implementation defect, local policy, version mismatch, semantic ambiguity, or unknown. Attribute the diagnosis and mark suspected causes as suspected. Record whether the workaround was reported, tested or remains proposed.

## Negative findings
Include supported failures with the same source requirements as successes. Missing evidence is not failure. Publication bias means collected cases cannot establish an industry success rate. Do not rank standards by raw case counts.

## Maintenance
Record reviewed_on, next_review, reviewer and correction history. A source URL being reachable does not verify its claims. Withdraw or supersede conclusions visibly when supporting evidence changes.

## Confidentiality
Use public sources or explicitly authorised summaries. Do not publish private correspondence, personal data, restricted standards text or client material without authority. Link to protected material only where permitted and disclose that readers may lack access.
