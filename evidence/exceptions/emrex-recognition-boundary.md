# EMREX: exchanging results does not complete academic recognition

## Boundary
The EMREX handbook distinguishes standardised record transfer from the additional information, shared classifications and local rules needed for more advanced automatic recognition.

- Cause category: local policy and semantic differences.
- Consequence: successful transfer is insufficient evidence of course equivalence.
- Response: local preparation of recognition rules and agreement on relevant information.
- Status: documented design boundary; not a failed DUO transaction.
- Cost and incidence: not established.
- Version: historical handbook; do not infer the current schema's full capabilities.
