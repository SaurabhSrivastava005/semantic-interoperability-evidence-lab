## Scope and reported outcome
Seto and colleagues describe two approaches to integrating clinical flowsheet measures. A simpler approach required less computation but offered limited research utility. Mapping to standard terminology, including LOINC, required more work and produced a dataset the authors considered more useful.

- Provisional outcome: partially-successful for the simpler approach; improved reported utility for the terminology-mapped approach.
- Source type: author-submitted research preprint.
- Version: OMOP release not established from the inspected abstract.
- Acceptance criteria, quantified effort and error rates: not available in this abstract review.

## Limits
This comparison supports a specific mapping-effort tradeoff. It does not establish comparative clinical safety or a general performance ranking. This record uses the abstract only; full-method and domain review remain pending.
