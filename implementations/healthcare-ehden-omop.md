## Scope and reported outcome
The EHDEN investigators report 210 harmonised data sources across 30 countries as of 1 September 2024. Their method includes funded ETL work, trained SMEs and quality inspection. This supports a bounded finding that a large harmonised network was established.

- Provisional SIEL outcome: successful-within-scope for network harmonisation.
- Source type: peer-reviewed descriptive study by programme participants.
- Standard: [OMOP CDM](../registry/omop-cdm.md); per-site versions not established here.
- Criteria: programme milestones and inspection; not a comparative ROI assessment.
- Costs and site-level maintenance effort: not extracted.
- Independent reproduction by SIEL: none.

## Limitations
The report describes repeated quality checks and site-specific vocabulary corrections. Network inclusion does not establish lossless mapping or suitability for every research question. Programme involvement is relevant to source independence.
