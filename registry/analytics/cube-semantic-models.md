## Scope, dependencies and evidence
Cube documents models using cubes, measures, dimensions and views. Views expose selected parts of the model to consumers.

This entry describes a modelling system. Each authored business model needs separate evidence for calculation correctness, joins, access controls, caching and consumer compatibility.
