## Scope, dependencies and evidence
Wikidata is a public structured knowledge resource, distinct from the Wikibase software used to maintain it. Catalogue a selected data snapshot or bounded extraction rather than assume a live query is immutable.

Its official copyright page places structured data in the main, Property, Lexeme and EntitySchema namespaces under CC0. Other namespace text has different terms.
