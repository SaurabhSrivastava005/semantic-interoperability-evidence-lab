## Scope, dependencies and evidence
DBpedia publishes a family of knowledge graph datasets. The graph data, its ontology and the software providing access are separate assets.

The official knowledge-graphs page identifies a Fusion beta under Business Source License 1.1. This must not be generalised to every DBpedia release, nor should historical open-data descriptions be applied to the Fusion beta.

Source: [official knowledge-graphs page](https://www.dbpedia.org/resources/knowledge-graphs/), Fusion Knowledge Graph beta section. See the [dataset-licensing exception](../../exceptions/dbpedia-dataset-licensing.md).

Before reuse, identify dataset, release, provenance and exact distribution terms. SIEL has not measured coverage, freshness or a downstream implementation result.
