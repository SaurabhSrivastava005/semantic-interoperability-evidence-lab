## Scope, dependencies and evidence
IEC 61970-301 describes utility-operation objects, attributes and relationships. The inspected consolidated edition is 2020 with Amendment 1:2022.

A CIM base model is not a complete exchange agreement. Identify the relevant companion specifications, exchange profile, model version and application requirements before comparing implementations.

The public IEC abstract is available; full normative content and redistribution terms were not inspected. No standard files are copied into SIEL.

Source: [official IEC listing](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/74467), title and abstract. The regulatory status of any proposed use is not assessed. No interoperability test or utility implementation result was evaluated.
