## Scope, dependencies and evidence
dbt documents semantic models with entities, dimensions and metrics used by MetricFlow. These definitions support analytical queries rather than constitute a prebuilt industry ontology.

The inspected page contains version-specific instructions, including v1.12-and-later sections. Pin the dbt and MetricFlow versions and inspect the deployed format rather than assume all examples are compatible.

Public documentation is not proof of a hosted-service entitlement or a single licence covering every engine release and authored model.

Source: [official semantic models guide](https://docs.getdbt.com/docs/build/semantic-models), configuration, entities and dimensions. No customer implementation or metric-correctness outcome was assessed.
