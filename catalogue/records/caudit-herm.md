## Purpose and version
HERM is a reference-model family, not a transaction protocol. The official page inspected lists v3.2.0 and describes a future v4 release; no newer release was verified.

## Licence and access
The License and Usage section names CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and adds statements restricting commercial use, bundling, sublicensing and passing material to other organisations. It also discusses customisation and sharing, with interpretation guidance in release notes.

Record both the licence and additional wording. The relationship between those statements has not been resolved here. Access to the full distribution may require institutional authentication or association access. SIEL has not inspected its release-note clarifications.

## SIEL treatment
Link to the official page and write original summaries. Do not bundle model files. Seek clarification for a planned reuse that the source terms do not clearly cover. This source check does not establish implementation effectiveness or permission for a particular commercial activity.
