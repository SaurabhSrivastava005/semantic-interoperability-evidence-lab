## Scope, dependencies and evidence
SAREF defines recurring smart-application concepts, relationships and axioms, with domain extensions. The inspected core page identifies v4.1.1 and publication on 31 October 2024.

A core ontology, a domain extension and a project-specific mapping are different dependencies. Record all selected versions. Standards-body publication does not by itself establish a local regulatory mandate.

The page references ETSI's IPR policy for extracted specification text. Full reuse and redistribution conditions for every ontology serialization were not established.

Source: [official v4.1.1 page](https://saref.etsi.org/core/v4.1.1/), version metadata, introduction and IPR notes. No multi-provider implementation or performance result was evaluated.
