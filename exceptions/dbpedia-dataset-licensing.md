## Boundary
The DBpedia Association's knowledge-graphs page identifies its Fusion beta as BSL 1.1. Do not infer a dataset's rights from the DBpedia name or a different release's terms.

## Response
Record the exact dataset distribution and applicable terms before reuse. Full BSL parameters and rights for every DBpedia dataset were not inspected; no blanket clearance is claimed.

Source: [official page](https://www.dbpedia.org/resources/knowledge-graphs/), Fusion Knowledge Graph beta section.
