# SIEL Charter
Version: 0.3  
Status: Draft for public review  
Proposed steward: Open Intelligence Institute

## Purpose
Provide a consolidated, accessible place to find relevant interoperability standards, understand industry exceptions and learn from documented implementation outcomes.

## Audience
Practitioners selecting standards, business and domain specialists interpreting differences, implementers investigating limitations, and decision-makers assessing evidence and adoption effort.

## Deliverables
- Industry guides and a structured standards catalogue.
- Use-case guides connecting business needs with candidate standards.
- Sourced implementation cases, including partial and unsuccessful outcomes.
- Exception records covering local differences, version conflicts, extensions and unresolved meaning.
- Transparent editorial decisions, corrections and source assessments.

## Boundaries
SIEL does not create a software platform, semantic kernel, universal ontology, normative industry model or certification scheme. It does not need to implement standards to catalogue reported experience. Research proposals can be linked but must remain separate from implementation evidence.

## Principles
1. Organise around user questions and bounded use cases.
2. Link to official stewards and identify versions and jurisdictions.
3. Separate source claims, contributor experience and editorial assessment.
4. Preserve exceptions and uncertainty rather than force equivalence.
5. Publish supported negative findings and allow responses and corrections.
6. Do not generalise one implementation into a verdict on an entire standard.
7. Record outcome and evidence strength separately.
8. Disclose conflicts of interest and reuse constraints.
9. Prefer useful, maintained records over catalogue size.
10. Do not infer failure from missing evidence.

## Coverage
Higher education and VET; healthcare and life sciences; financial services; energy and utilities; real estate and construction; government and public services; manufacturing and supply chain; retail and consumer. These are discovery areas, not claims of validated industry coverage.

## Success measures
Track reviewed records by industry and use case, the proportion with versioned official references, the proportion linking implementation evidence and exceptions, stale records, correction turnaround and user feedback on whether the hub helped a selection decision. Publish counts with definitions. Do not report an industry success rate from a selectively collected set of cases.

## Maintenance and authority
Follow [governance](GOVERNANCE.md) and the [evidence policy](docs/evidence-policy.md). Editorial acceptance means that a record meets publication requirements, not that SIEL endorses a product or certifies an implementation.
